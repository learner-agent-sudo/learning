# U.S. legislation extracts

Do not edit by hand. The text of the U.S. Code and California code sections
the software IP volume cites, held in the same shape as `data/caleg` so
`scripts/build_statute_extracts.py` can attach it to citations such as
`{"cap": "17-USC", "section": "106"}` or `{"cap": "17-USC", "section": "101 \"computer program\""}`.

Sources:
- `15-USC`, `17-USC`, `18-USC`, `19-USC`, `28-USC`, `35-USC`: generated by
  `scripts/extract_uscode.py` (run monthly by `.github/workflows/refresh-usleg.yml`)
  from the latest annual edition of the United States Code that the Office of
  the Law Revision Counsel prepares and the U.S. Government Publishing Office
  publishes on GovInfo.
- `CA-LAB`, `CA-BPC`, `CA-CIV`: California Labor, Business and Professions, and
  Civil Code sections, copied from California Legislative Information as
  indexed in April 2026 (written by hand; re-copy when the law changes).

Works of the United States Government are not subject to copyright
(17 U.S.C. 105). These extracts are **not** the official text. For that, see
uscode.house.gov, govinfo.gov and leginfo.legislature.ca.gov.
