# U.S. legislation extracts

Generated by `scripts/extract_uslaw.py` (run weekly by
`.github/workflows/refresh-usleg.yml`), except the state statutes noted
below. Do not edit the generated files by hand.

Sources:
- The United States Code in USLM XML, from the Office of the Law Revision
  Counsel of the House of Representatives (uscode.house.gov), at the current
  release point named in each file.
- The Electronic Code of Federal Regulations (ecfr.gov), through its public
  versioner API.

Works of the United States Government are not subject to copyright
(17 U.S.C. 105). These extracts are **not** the official text. For that, see
uscode.house.gov, govinfo.gov and ecfr.gov.
