# Canadian legislation extracts

Generated by `scripts/extract_justice.py` (run weekly by
`.github/workflows/refresh-caleg.yml`). Do not edit by hand.

Source: the consolidated Acts and regulations of Canada published by the
Department of Justice Canada as XML (github.com/justicecanada/laws-lois-xml,
the data behind the Justice Laws Website). Federal enactments may be
reproduced without charge or permission under the Reproduction of Federal Law
Order (SI/97-5), provided due diligence is exercised in ensuring the accuracy
of the materials reproduced and the reproduction is not represented as an
official version.

These extracts are **not** the official version. For the official text, see
https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca.
